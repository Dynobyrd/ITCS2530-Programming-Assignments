## Interest
My passion for dinosaurs and paleontology heavily influences my approach to game development and creative programming. I enjoy ventures that allow me to blend prehistoric worldbuilding with interactivity and storytelling. In regards to any creative tasks, I like to include dinosaurs wherever I can and I am excited to do so in this course!

I feel it is difficult for me to choose a favorite with so many prehistoric reptiles unearthed, but I commonly make use of the brontosaurus in my projects. Reflecting upon seeing Jurassic Park so many times, consistently stunned by their regal presence and wishing I could feed my very own brachiosaurus, I've held a special place in my heart for sauropods for as long as I can remember.
